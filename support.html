How it works

Four things to know about KeebsforWater.

Student-crafted keyboards

Built by hand

The team hand-builds, tunes and customizes mechanical keyboards. That means lubing switches, custom plates, acoustic dampening and stabilizer tuning.

100% of profit to clean water

Zero administrative cut

After covering component and build costs, 100% of remaining profits go straight into water filtration development.

The crisis

Arsenic in Bangladesh groundwater

Groundwater supplies roughly 97% of drinking water in Bangladesh, and nearly half of tested wells exceed the WHO guideline of 10 µg/L.

The solution

PureHusk

Profits fund research and prototyping of a low-cost household filtration solution built from locally sourced materials and straightforward upkeep.

Transparency

Research, stated plainly.

PureHusk is active research in progress. The team is coordinating with university laboratories for atomic spectrometry testing, to validate arsenic reduction against WHO guidelines before any field deployment.
