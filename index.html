The idea

A farm leftover, put to work.

Rice husk is a waste product that is usually dumped or burned. Processed carefully, it becomes the part of the filter that grabs arsenic.

Step 1

Rice husk

Bangladesh grows about 55 million metric tons of rice a year, and roughly 20% of each grain by weight is husk.

Step 2

Controlled ash

Heated at a controlled temperature, the husk keeps its silica amorphous and becomes porous. Open burning does the opposite.

Step 3

Iron-coated

Arsenic binds to iron oxide, not to plain ash. The coating is what turns husk ash into a working filter medium.
